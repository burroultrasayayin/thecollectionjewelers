How to buy

A short process, because these pieces are not all in one place.

  1. Browse the collection and choose a watch you would like to reserve. Prices are available by inquiry only.
  2. Contact us through the form or through Facebook to reserve the piece. Every watch must be reserved before it is shown or sold.
  3. Payment is required in advance. Watches are stored in different locations, so a reservation and prepaid payment come first.
  4. Once the reservation and payment are confirmed, we will arrange for the piece to be shown or delivered as agreed.
